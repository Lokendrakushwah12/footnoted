One question takes eight steps

  1. Chunk. Pages are split on headings into pieces of at most 1,200 characters.
  2. Index. 31,777 chunks go into SQLite for keyword search and a matrix of vectors.
  3. Retrieve. The two rankings are fused, at most two chunks from any page.
  4. Budget. Instructions, question and passages are fitted to the model’s window.
  5. Draft. The model writes claims, each with a passage number and a quote.
  6. Ground. A claim is kept only if its quote’s words appear, in order, in a passage.
  7. Verify. A second call checks that the claims answer the question.
  8. Answer or decline. Nothing left means two rewritten searches, then a refusal.

1,438 lines of Python. The index is one SQLite file and one array. The model is Qwen 2.5 3B, served by Ollama. There is no framework, vector database or API key.

Around the loop: a three-tier memory that rewrites follow-up questions, a knowledge graph of the MCP specification, and a gate in CI that fails a change which makes the answers worse.

What it costs to run

Nothing was spent. The model is free and local, the index is two 47 MB files, and no hosted service is involved. Token counts per question were not recorded, so there is no per-question price to quote; the largest prompt was 1,886 tokens and replies are capped at 600.

Of 84 questions, 34 were settled by the first search, 17 needed one rewrite and 33 used all three attempts.

The check that runs in CI needs no model. It compares a recorded scorecard with the accepted one.

What this is not evidence of

  • footnoted has no users. Its effect is what the measurements show.
  • The model is the ceiling: 60 good searches become 31 grounded answers. A larger model is untested.
  • The evals are small and were written by the builder, and the search settings were tuned on the questions they are reported on.
  • The gate’s model judge agreed with a key-term check on 26 of 34 answers, so it is reported and never gated on.

Re-recording unchanged code moved the score by one question and passed the gate. Cutting search to one passage moved it from 31 to 19 and failed, naming each question that got worse.
